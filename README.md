# Make It to the Moon

A little alien crash-landed in a junkyard on Earth. Home is up on the Moon.
Turn a kitchen chair, some planks and a bundle of fireworks into a rocket, fly higher every day, and make it home.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser, or play the hosted version on GitHub Pages.

## How to play

- Press **START**, then **hold W, the up arrow, or Space** to fire the engine. Let go to save fuel.
- **A / D or the arrow keys** slide the rocket left and right.
- Once you build a Launch Spring, it fires by itself every launch.
- Each flight is one day. Get paid for how high you fly, then spend your cash on upgrades in the shop.
- Challenges show up on day 3. Find the hidden rainbow star in the sky to unlock Rainbow Star Challenges, which pay 1-3 stars each. Spend 5 on the Mystery Box to roll a random cosmetic (golden ones are super rare), then dress up your rocket in the Inventory.
- Dodge birds, planes, balloons, drones, asteroids and space junk. In Deep Space, giant asteroids destroy you on contact.
- Land on the Moon to unlock Infinite Mode: the engine never stops, waves of space traffic, an alien boss, and planets to fly past. Drop to 50% power and you explode.

Everything runs in a single HTML file. Saves are kept in your browser.
